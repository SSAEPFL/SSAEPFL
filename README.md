# An optical way to track satellites

Founded at EPFL in 2020, the Space Situational Awareness (SSA) EPFL Team aims at developing expertise and tools in the field of space situational awareness by using our telescopes to track and characterize the orbits and attitudes of objects in space. Our mid-term goal is to observe, identify, and study as many satellites and debris as possible from the ground.
